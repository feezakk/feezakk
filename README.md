# Feeza Khan Khanzada

PhD Candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the
University of Michigan-Dearborn.

I conduct research in autonomous driving, driving world models,
model-based reinforcement learning, robotics, and simulation-based learning.

## Research Interests

- Autonomous Driving
- Driving World Models
- Model-Based Reinforcement Learning
- Reinforcement Learning
- CARLA Simulation
- Robotics
- Action-Conditioned Prediction
- Multimodal Representation Learning

## Selected Research

- BranchDrive — Action-Conditioned Driving Prediction
- InDRiVE — Reward-Free World-Model Pretraining
- D2S-WMD — World-Model Distillation
- Camera-First Waypoint Prediction
